Providing hope and relief with compassion, dignity and respect

The Community Table is a food bank located in Riverside South, run 100% by volunteers. Our goal is to serve familes in Riverside South with a hope to grow to include our neighbouring communities.

We are a faith-based charitable organization that extends a warm welcome to anyone struggling to provide food for their familes. According to Stats Canada, there are over 600 households in Riverside South living below the poverty line.

Make a difference, volunteer!

We are grateful for our passionate volunteers. New volunteers are always welcome to collect and sort food, provide general administration, and help serve our neighbours.
